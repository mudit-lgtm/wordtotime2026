📚 Public Speaking Time: Complete Guide

How long is a speech based on word count?

A speech at the standard public speaking pace of 130 WPM (per ASHA) runs approximately 7.7 seconds per 16 words. The most practical formula: Minutes = Word Count ÷ 130. For a 1200-word speech: 1200 ÷ 130 = 9.23 minutes = 9 minutes 14 seconds. Use the calculator above to avoid doing the math manually.

How many words per minute for public speaking?

The standard words per minute rate for public speaking is 130 WPM (ASHA). Toastmasters International recommends 120–160 WPM as the effective public speaking range. TED Talk speakers average 163 WPM. For complex or technical content, slow to 110–120 WPM for comprehension. For energetic storytelling, 150–165 WPM maintains engagement. See our full WPM guide for all speaking contexts.

1200 Words Fact: A 1200-word speech runs 9m 14s at 130 WPM — perfect for a typical 8–10 minute speaking slot. Add 10–15% extra time for natural pauses, emphasis, and audience interaction: total actual delivery ≈ 10–11 minutes.

What is a speech timer and how does it work?

A speech timer is a countdown clock used during speech practice or competition to track elapsed time versus your assigned slot. Our live speech timer above mirrors the Toastmasters traffic-light system: green light (under 80% of slot time) means you're on track, yellow (80–100%) means you're in the zone, and red (over time) means you've exceeded your slot. This real-time visual feedback is far more useful than watching a clock.

How to use the live countdown timer

  • Enter your assigned speaking slot in minutes (e.g., 7 for a 7-minute speech)
  • Optionally name your speech so you can identify it during practice
  • Click Start Timer and begin speaking
  • Watch the traffic light: green = on pace, yellow = approaching limit, red = over time
  • Use Pause to stop mid-practice (e.g., for notes), then resume
  • Combine with the practice mode for full rehearsal tracking
For Conference Presentations: Most conference slots are 15–20 minutes. At 130 WPM: 15 minutes = ~1,950 words, 20 minutes = ~2,600 words. Always write to 90% of slot length to allow for pauses and questions. Use our presentation time calculator for slide-by-slide timing.

Speech duration by slot type

  • 2-minute speech: ~260 words

  • 7-minute speech: ~910 words
  • 10-minute speech: ~1,300 words
