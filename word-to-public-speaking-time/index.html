Frequently Asked Questions

An 8-minute speech at 130 WPM = 1,040 words. At slow pace (110 WPM): 880 words. At fast pace (150 WPM): 1,200 words.
TED Talks are limited to 18 minutes and average 163 WPM, giving approximately 2,934 words for a full-length TED Talk. See the speaking speed guide for more context.
For formal occasions (keynotes, ceremonies), aim for 110–120 WPM. This pace conveys authority. Per the NSDA.
