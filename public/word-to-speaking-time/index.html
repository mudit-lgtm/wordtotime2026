About Speaking Speed

Your speaking speed determines how much content you can cover in any time slot. Per ASHA, the average person speaks at 130 WPM in professional settings. Always account for pauses — a 1,000-word speech calculated at 7m 41s typically runs 8–8.5 minutes in practice.

For presentations with slides, use the presentation calculator which automatically accounts for pauses. For formal public events see the public speaking timer. Measure your personal WPM with the Practice Mode timer.

Frequently Asked Questions

At 130 WPM (average), 1,000 words takes 7 minutes 41 seconds. At 110 WPM: 9m 5s. At 150 WPM: 6m 40s. See 1,000 words page.
500 words takes 3 minutes 51 seconds at 130 WPM. Visit 500 words to minutes.
2,000 words takes 15 minutes 23 seconds at 130 WPM. See 2,000 words to minutes.
900 words takes approximately 6 minutes 55 seconds at 130 WPM. See 900 words to minutes.
For most speeches, aim for 120–140 WPM. Below 100 WPM feels monotonous; above 160 WPM risks losing your audience. Use the practice timer to calibrate.
The calculator estimates pure speech time without pauses. In practice, pauses add 10–20%. Use the presentation calculator (100 WPM effective rate) to automatically include pause time.
