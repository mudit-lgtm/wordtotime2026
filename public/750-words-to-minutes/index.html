FAQ — 750 Words

A 750-word speech takes 5m 46s at average pace (130 WPM). Slow (110 WPM): 6m 49s. Fast (150 WPM): 5m 00s.
750 words takes 3m 09s to read silently (238 WPM), or 4m 06s to read aloud (183 WPM). Compare with reading time calculator.
750 words is approximately 1.5 pages single-spaced (500 words/page), or 3.0 pages double-spaced.
For a presentation with slides, 750 words takes approximately 7m 30s (100 WPM effective rate, includes transitions and pauses). See the presentation timer.
