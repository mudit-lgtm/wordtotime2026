Writing a 5-Minute Speech

To write a 5-minute speech, aim for approximately 650 words at 130 WPM. If you're unsure of your speaking speed, use the Practice Mode timer to measure it first, then use "Custom" speed in the calculator above for personalized results.

Write to 585 words (~90% of target), then add back emphasis and transitions during rehearsal. Most speakers slow down under pressure, so the buffer prevents rushing. For data on ideal speaking rates, see the speaking speed guide.

FAQ — 5-Minute Speech

A 5-minute speech needs approximately 650 words at average pace (130 WPM). At slow pace (110 WPM): 550 words. At fast pace (150 WPM): 750 words.
Aim for 650 words. Write to 585 words (~10% buffer) to allow room for natural pauses and nerves that slow delivery. Use the Practice Mode timer to rehearse.
Start with a clear single message. Use the structure: Hook (10%) → Context (20%) → Main Point (50%) → Story/Example (10%) → Call to Action (10%). Aim for 650 words total, and practice at least 3 times before delivery.
