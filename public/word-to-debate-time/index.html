Frequently Asked Questions

Standard debate speaking runs 150–170 WPM per NSDA. Policy debate "spreading" can reach 200–250 WPM.
At 150 WPM, an 8-minute speech uses approximately 1,200 words. See also the speaking time calculator.
A debate break calculator determines wins needed to advance to elimination rounds. At most tournaments, breaking requires winning 60–75% of preliminary rounds. Contact your tournament director for thresholds.
