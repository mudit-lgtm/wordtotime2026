About Average Speaking Rate

The average speaking rate for North American English is approximately 130 WPM in formal settings, based on research from the ASHA. For competitive debaters, the NSDA recommends 120–150 WPM for most formats. TED Talks average 163 WPM — higher than standard to maintain energy within the strict 18-minute format.

To measure your personal speaking rate, use our Practice Mode timer. Most people speak either faster or slower than the 130 WPM default, so measuring your own rate gives you more accurate predictions from the words to time calculator and the words to minutes calculator.
