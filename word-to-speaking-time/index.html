Frequently Asked Questions

Speaking 1,000 words aloud takes 7 minutes 41 seconds at the average professional speaking rate of 130 WPM. Slow (110 WPM): 9m 5s. Fast (150 WPM): 6m 40s.

500 words takes 3 minutes 51 seconds to speak at 130 WPM. This is about the length of a short toast or brief class presentation.

2,000 words takes 15 minutes 23 seconds to speak at 130 WPM. At 110 WPM: 18m 11s. At 150 WPM: 13m 20s.

900 words takes 6 minutes 55 seconds to speak at the average pace of 130 WPM. At 110 WPM: 8m 11s. At 150 WPM: 6m 0s.

For formal speeches, aim for 120–140 WPM. TED Talks average 163 WPM. Conversational content works well at 150–170 WPM. Ceremonial speeches often use 100–120 WPM for gravitas.

The calculator gives you scripted word time. Add 10–15% for natural pauses, audience reaction, and emphasis. For a 1,000-word speech, budget 8m 30s–9m 0s total for a 130 WPM speaker.
