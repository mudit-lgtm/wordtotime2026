🎯 Speaking Pace by Context

Different speaking situations call for different paces. Use the right WPM preset for accurate estimates.

🏭
Formal Speech / Lecture
100–120 WPM
Slow, deliberate delivery for complex content. Allows audience to absorb information. Common in academic and political settings.
Use: 110 WPM preset
🎤
Standard Speech / Presentation
120–140 WPM
Average comfortable delivery. Recommended by Toastmasters International for most presentations. Balances clarity and engagement.
Use: 130 WPM preset ✓
🌟
TED Talk
163 WPM
Research on TED Talks shows the average is 163 WPM. Engaging, energetic pace. Used by inspiring speakers like Simon Sinek and Brené Brown.
Use: 163 WPM preset
🎧
Podcast / Conversational
150–180 WPM
Natural conversational pace. Podcasts and audiobooks average 150–160 WPM. Fast talkers or high-energy content may reach 180 WPM.
Use: 150–180 WPM custom
🗣
Audiobook Narration
150–160 WPM
Professional audiobook narration averages 150–160 WPM for fiction, and 140–150 WPM for non-fiction. Varies by genre and narrator style.
Use: 150 WPM conversational
📺
News Broadcast
160–180 WPM
TV and radio news anchors typically speak at 160–180 WPM. Designed for continuous information delivery with high clarity.
Use: custom 170 WPM
Pro Tip: Always add a 10–15% buffer to your word count for pauses, emphasis, and audience interaction. For a 5-minute slot, aim for 4 minutes 30 seconds of speaking time with buffer. Use our speech length calculator to check if your script fits your slot.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to say 500 words?+
500 words takes 3 minutes 51 seconds to say at the average speaking pace of 130 WPM per ASHA. At formal slow pace (110 WPM): 4 minutes 33 seconds. At conversational pace (150 WPM): 3 minutes 20 seconds. At TED Talk pace (163 WPM): 3 minutes 4 seconds. Use the calculator above for your exact pace. See our 500 words to minutes guide for full details.
How long does it take to speak 1,000 words?+
1,000 words takes 7 minutes 41 seconds to speak at 130 WPM average. At slow formal pace (110 WPM): 9 minutes 5 seconds. At conversational pace (150 WPM): 6 minutes 40 seconds. At TED Talk pace (163 WPM): 6 minutes 8 seconds. See our 1,000 words to minutes guide.
How long is a 500-word speech?+
A 500-word speech is 3 to 4 minutes long depending on pace. At 130 WPM average: 3 minutes 51 seconds. At slow formal delivery (110 WPM): 4 minutes 33 seconds. Always add 10% buffer time for pauses, emphasis and audience interaction. See our 5-minute speech word count guide for ideal script length.
How long is a 1,000-word speech?+
A 1,000-word speech is 7 to 9 minutes long. At 130 WPM average: 7 minutes 41 seconds. At slow formal delivery (110 WPM): 9 minutes 5 seconds. At conversational pace (150 WPM): 6 minutes 40 seconds. This is typical for a standard 8-minute keynote segment. Check our 10-minute speech word count guide for ideal length.
How long does it take to say 400 words?+
400 words takes 3 minutes 5 seconds at 130 WPM average. At slow formal pace (110 WPM): 3 minutes 38 seconds. At conversational pace (150 WPM): 2 minutes 40 seconds. Common for award introductions, elevator pitches, and short classroom presentations. See our 400 words to minutes guide.
How long does it take to say 1,500 words?+
1,500 words takes 11 minutes 32 seconds at 130 WPM average. At 110 WPM: 13 minutes 38 seconds. At 150 WPM: 10 minutes exactly. At TED Talk pace (163 WPM): 9 minutes 12 seconds. See our 1,500 words to minutes guide and 15-minute speech word count guide.
How long does it take to speak 2,000 words?+
2,000 words takes 15 minutes 23 seconds at 130 WPM average. At 110 WPM: 18 minutes 11 seconds. At 150 WPM: 13 minutes 20 seconds. At TED Talk pace (163 WPM): 12 minutes 17 seconds. This is typical for a standard 15-minute conference presentation. See our 2,000 words to minutes guide.
How many words per minute in a speech?+
A speech averages 130 words per minute for formal delivery per ASHA. Toastmasters recommends 120–160 WPM for most speeches. TED Talks average 163 WPM. News broadcasters speak at 160–180 WPM. Academic lectures average 100–120 WPM for maximum clarity. Visit our speaking words per minute guide for a complete breakdown.

How many minutes is a 1,000-word speech?+
