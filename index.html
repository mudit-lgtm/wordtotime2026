❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to say 500 words?+
500 words takes 3 minutes 51 seconds at the average speaking pace of 130 WPM. At a slow formal pace (110 WPM): 4 minutes 33 seconds. At conversational pace (150 WPM): 3 minutes 20 seconds. Use our words to minutes calculator to get a precise result at your own pace. See also: 500 words to minutes complete guide.
How long does it take to speak 1,000 words?+
1,000 words takes 7 minutes 41 seconds to speak at the average pace of 130 WPM. At slow pace (110 WPM): 9 minutes 5 seconds. At fast pace (150 WPM): 6 minutes 40 seconds. See our full 1,000 words to minutes guide for more breakdowns.
How many minutes is 1,000 words?+
1,000 words is 7 minutes 41 seconds when spoken at average pace (130 WPM) and 4 minutes 12 seconds when read silently (238 WPM). For a presentation with pauses, allow 8–9 minutes. Use our words to minutes calculator for custom WPM settings.
How many words is a 5-minute speech?+
A 5-minute speech is approximately 650 words at 130 WPM average. At fast pace (150 WPM): 750 words. At slow pace (110 WPM): 550 words. See our dedicated 5-minute speech word count guide for tips, examples and a full script template.
How many words is a 10-minute speech?+
A 10-minute speech is approximately 1,300 words at 130 WPM average. At 150 WPM: 1,500 words. Always add 10% buffer for pauses, emphasis, and audience interaction. See our 10-minute speech word count guide for full details.
How many words is a 3-minute speech?+
A 3-minute speech is approximately 390 words at 130 WPM average. Range: 330–450 words depending on speaking pace. Common for wedding toasts, award introductions, and short conference presentations. See our 3-minute speech word count guide.
How long is a 500-word speech?+
A 500-word speech takes 3 minutes 51 seconds at 130 WPM average. This is a typical length for a 3–4 minute keynote segment, a wedding toast, or a short class presentation. Use the words to minutes calculator above to adjust for your exact pace.
How many words is a 4-minute speech?+
A 4-minute speech is approximately 520 words at 130 WPM average. Range: 440–600 words depending on delivery speed. See our 4-minute speech word count guide.
How many words is 8 minutes?+
8 minutes of speaking equals approximately 1,040 words at 130 WPM average. At 150 WPM: 1,200 words. At 110 WPM: 880 words. Use the reverse calculator tab above: enter 8 minutes and your preferred speaking pace to get an exact word count.
How many words is 4 minutes?+
4 minutes of speaking equals approximately 520 words at 130 WPM average. At 150 WPM: 600 words. At 110 WPM: 440 words. Use the reverse calculator tab above to calculate your target word count from any time duration.
