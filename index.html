How the Words to Time Calculator Works

The calculation is: Time (minutes) = Word count ÷ WPM. At 130 WPM, 1,000 words = 7.69 minutes = 7 minutes 41 seconds. Our calculator defaults to 130 WPM — the most cited average for prepared speeches, validated by the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association (ASHA). Adjust using the speed selector, or enter a custom rate after measuring yourself in Practice Mode.

Four Timing Modes — What Each Result Means

  • 🎤 Speaking time — Delivers your text as a prepared speech at your chosen WPM. Most important for speech and presentation planning.
  • 👁 Silent reading — How long someone reads your text silently at 238 WPM. Use for blog post and article reading time estimates.
  • 📢 Read aloud — Reading your text out loud at 183 WPM (oral reading average). Use for audiobooks, podcasts, or voiceover scripts.
  • 📊 Presentation — Uses ~100 WPM effective rate to account for slide transitions, pauses, and live delivery slowdown. See the presentation time calculator for details.

Speaking Speed vs. Reading Speed

Many people confuse these. They are fundamentally different processes:

ModeAvg SpeedUse case
Silent reading238 WPMArticles, blog posts, essays
Read aloud183 WPMAudiobooks, read-aloud content
Prepared speech130 WPMSpeeches, presentations
TED Talk style~163 WPMRehearsed, energetic delivery
Formal lecture100–120 WPMDeliberate, allows note-taking
Competitive debate170–250+ WPMArgument-dense, specialist

The National Speech & Debate Association (NSDA) recommends 130 WPM for most formal speaking. See our full speaking words per minute guide.

Word Count for Common Content Types

  • Elevator pitch: 30–60 seconds → 65–130 words → ~200 words max
  • Wedding / best man speech: 3–5 min → 390–650 words
  • Classroom presentation: 5–10 min → 650–1,300 words
  • TED Talk (max 18 min): ~2,340 words · 1,500 words = 11m 32s
  • Conference keynote (30–45 min): 3,900–5,850 words
  • Blog post (4–8 min read): 1,000–2,000 words
  • Podcast / audiobook: 150–165 WPM — use the Fast speed setting

Saying 1,000 words out loud takes approximately 7 minutes 41 seconds at 130 WPM average. For a TED-style energetic delivery at 163 WPM: about 6 minutes 8 seconds. For oral reading (audiobook style) at 183 WPM: 5 minutes 27 seconds. Full breakdown at our speech length calculator.
