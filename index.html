How to Convert Words to Speaking Time

The Formula

The formula for converting word count to speaking time is straightforward: Time (seconds) = Word Count ÷ WPM × 60. For a 1,000-word speech at 130 WPM: 1,000 ÷ 130 × 60 = 461 seconds = 7 minutes 41 seconds.

The key variable is your words-per-minute (WPM) rate, which varies significantly by context, speaker, and delivery style.

Reading Speed vs Speaking Speed

There's an important distinction between reading silently, reading aloud, and speaking from memory:

TypeAverage WPMUse Case
Silent reading238 WPMBlog posts, articles, books
Read aloud183 WPMAudiobooks, narration, poetry
Average speaking130 WPMFormal speeches, presentations
Conversational150–170 WPMPodcasts, interviews, casual talks

Pro Tips for Speech Timing

💡 Expert Advice
  • Add 10–15% buffer — live pauses, audience reaction, and nerves slow you down
  • Practice out loud — reading in your head is always faster than speaking aloud
  • Mark pause points in your script to allow natural breathing and emphasis
  • Record yourself — use Practice Mode to measure your actual WPM
  • Target 130 WPM for formal speeches, 150–160 for conversational content
