Frequently Asked Questions

A 1,000-word speech takes 7 minutes 41 seconds at the average speaking pace of 130 WPM. At 110 WPM: 9m 5s. At 150 WPM: 6m 40s.

Saying 1,000 words aloud takes 7 minutes 41 seconds at 130 WPM (average), per ASHA research. Add 10–15% buffer for natural pauses.

A 500-word speech takes 3 minutes 51 seconds at 130 WPM. This is a common length for wedding toasts, class introductions, and brief remarks.

A 700-word speech takes 5 minutes 23 seconds at 130 WPM. At 110 WPM: 6m 22s. At 150 WPM: 4m 40s.

A 2,000-word speech takes 15 minutes 23 seconds at 130 WPM. This is a common length for keynote presentations and academic lectures.

Toast/remarks: 1–3 min (130–390 words). Wedding speech: 3–5 min (390–650 words). TEDx: 10–18 min. Keynote: 20–60 min. Academic lecture: 50–60 min.
