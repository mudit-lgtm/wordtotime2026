❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How long is a 500-word speech?+
A 500-word speech is 3 minutes 51 seconds at the average speaking pace of 130 WPM per ASHA. At formal slow pace (110 WPM): 4 minutes 33 seconds. At conversational pace (150 WPM): 3 minutes 20 seconds. At TED Talk pace (163 WPM): 3 minutes 4 seconds. See our 500 words to minutes guide for complete details.
How long is a 1,000-word speech?+
A 1,000-word speech is 7 minutes 41 seconds at 130 WPM average. At slow formal pace (110 WPM): 9 minutes 5 seconds. At conversational pace (150 WPM): 6 minutes 40 seconds. At TED Talk pace (163 WPM): 6 minutes 8 seconds. See our 1,000 words to minutes guide.
How many words is a 4-minute speech?+
A 4-minute speech requires approximately 520 words at the average speaking pace of 130 WPM. At slow formal pace (110 WPM): 440 words. At conversational pace (150 WPM): 600 words. At TED Talk pace (163 WPM): 652 words. See our full 4-minute speech word count guide.
How many words is an 8-minute speech?+
An 8-minute speech requires approximately 1,040 words at 130 WPM average. At slow formal pace (110 WPM): 880 words. At conversational pace (150 WPM): 1,200 words. At TED Talk pace (163 WPM): 1,304 words. Use the reverse calculator above to get the exact count for your pace.
How many words is a 7-minute speech?+
A 7-minute speech requires approximately 910 words at 130 WPM average. At slow formal pace (110 WPM): 770 words. At conversational pace (150 WPM): 1,050 words. At TED Talk pace (163 WPM): 1,141 words. See our complete 7-minute speech word count guide.
How many words is a 5-minute speech?+
A 5-minute speech requires approximately 650 words at 130 WPM average. At slow formal pace (110 WPM): 550 words. At conversational pace (150 WPM): 750 words. At TED Talk pace (163 WPM): 815 words. See our comprehensive 5-minute speech word count guide.
How long does it take to say 1,000 words?+
It takes 7 minutes 41 seconds to say 1,000 words at the average speaking pace of 130 WPM. At slow formal delivery (110 WPM): 9 minutes 5 seconds. At conversational pace (150 WPM): 6 minutes 40 seconds. At TED Talk pace (163 WPM): 6 minutes 8 seconds. Use our speaking time calculator for any word count.
How long is a speech in minutes for different word counts?+
At 130 WPM average: 200 words = 1m 32s, 300 words = 2m 18s, 400 words = 3m 05s, 500 words = 3m 51s, 750 words = 5m 46s, 1,000 words = 7m 41s, 1,500 words = 11m 32s, 2,000 words = 15m 23s, 3,000 words = 23m 04s. See the complete table above or use the words to minutes calculator.
How do I know if my script is too long?+
Use the Slot Fit Checker tab above — enter your word count, your assigned time slot, and your speaking pace. You'll get a color-coded verdict: ✅ Perfect fit, ⚠️ Close (trim 10%), 🔴 Too long (cut significantly), or 🔵 Too short (add content). A safe rule: your speech text should time to 90% of your slot to leave room for pauses and applause.
What is the average speaking rate for a speech?+
The average speaking rate for a formal speech is 130 WPM per ASHA. Toastmasters International recommends 120–160 WPM for most presentations. TED Talks average 163 WPM. Academic lectures average 100–120 WPM. Visit our speaking words per minute guide for full WPM research.
