How to Use Practice Mode

  1. Paste your speech or text into the text area above.
  2. Click "Start Timer" and immediately begin reading at your natural pace.
  3. Click "Stop" when you finish reading the entire text.
  4. Read your WPM result — shown live as you read.
  5. Use your WPM in any calculator above by selecting "Custom" speed for personalized results.

Also see our dedicated free reading speed test, or compare your results on the speaking words per minute guide. To convert your word count to time, go to the main calculator or the words to minutes tool.
