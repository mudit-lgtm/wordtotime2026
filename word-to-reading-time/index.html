What is the Average Reading Speed?

According to research published by the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association, the average adult reads silently at 238 words per minute with good comprehension. Reading aloud averages 183 WPM.

These rates vary by text complexity, reading purpose, and individual skill level. Technical or academic text typically reads 15–25% slower than casual content.

📖 Reading Speed by Context
  • Casual reading (novels, blogs): 250–300 WPM
  • Academic text (textbooks, papers): 150–200 WPM
  • Average adult (all types): ~238 WPM
  • Read aloud (narration, audiobook): ~183 WPM
  • Speed reading: 400–700 WPM (comprehension drops)
