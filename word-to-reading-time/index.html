Reading Speed Profiles

Your actual reading speed significantly affects the result. Use the right WPM for accurate estimates.

Child Reader
75–150 WPM
Elementary school students. Simple texts at 75 WPM, improving to 150 by grade 6.
Reading Aloud
110–150 WPM
Average speaking rate per ASHA. Use 130 WPM for speeches, audiobooks, and read-alouds.
Average Adult
238 WPM
Default silent reading speed per National Reading Panel. Most adults read at 200–280 WPM silently.
Proficient
280–350 WPM
College graduates, avid readers. Good comprehension maintained at this speed.
Speed Reader
700+ WPM
Trained speed readers. Comprehension may drop below 500 WPM for complex content.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to read 1,000 words?+
1,000 words takes 4 minutes 12 seconds to read silently at the average reading speed of 238 WPM. If you’re reading aloud at 130 WPM, it takes 7 minutes 41 seconds. A slow reader at 150 WPM will take about 6 minutes 40 seconds. Use the calculator above for your specific reading speed. See our 1,000 words to minutes page for a detailed guide.
How long does it take to read 2,000 words?+
2,000 words takes 8 minutes 24 seconds to read silently at 238 WPM. Reading aloud takes 15 minutes 23 seconds at 130 WPM. A fast reader at 350 WPM takes about 5 minutes 43 seconds. This is a typical length for a long blog post or detailed how-to guide. See our 2,000 words to minutes guide.
How long does it take to read 500 words?+
500 words takes 2 minutes 6 seconds to read silently at 238 WPM. Reading aloud takes 3 minutes 51 seconds at 130 WPM. This is the typical length of a short blog post, news article, or product description. See our 500 words to minutes page.
How long does it take to read 3,000 words?+
3,000 words takes 12 minutes 36 seconds silently at 238 WPM. Reading aloud takes 23 minutes 4 seconds at 130 WPM. This is common for long-form articles, case studies, and detailed guides. A fast reader (350 WPM) finishes in about 8 minutes 34 seconds.
How long does it take to read 1,500 words?+
1,500 words takes 6 minutes 18 seconds silently at 238 WPM. Reading aloud takes 11 minutes 32 seconds at 130 WPM. This is a typical medium-length blog post. See our 1,500 words to minutes guide.
How long does it take to read 2,500 words?+
2,500 words takes 10 minutes 30 seconds silently at 238 WPM. Reading aloud takes 19 minutes 14 seconds at 130 WPM. Common for comprehensive articles and research summaries. See our 2,500 words to minutes guide.
What is the average reading speed?+
The average adult reads silently at 238 WPM, based on research by the National Reading Panel. Reading aloud averages 130 WPM per ASHA. Speed readers reach 700+ WPM. Children read 75–150 WPM. College graduates typically read 280–350 WPM silently.
How do I calculate reading time from word count?+
Use the formula: Reading Time (seconds) = Word Count ÷ WPM × 60. For silent reading use 238 WPM; for reading aloud use 130 WPM. Example: 1,000 words ÷ 238 × 60 = 252 seconds = 4 minutes 12 seconds. Or just use the free calculator above for instant results.
How long does it take to read 1,000 words out loud?+
Reading 1,000 words out loud takes 7 minutes 41 seconds at the average speaking pace of 130 WPM. At a slow pace of 110 WPM it takes 9 minutes 5 seconds. This is relevant for audiobooks, speeches, and classroom read-alouds. Use our speaking time calculator for speech-specific estimates.
How long does it take to read 2,000 words out loud?+
Reading 2,000 words out loud takes 15 minutes 23 seconds at 130 WPM average speaking pace. At slow pace (110 WPM): 18 minutes 11 seconds. For presentations, always add 10% buffer time for pauses and transitions.
How long does it take to read 4,000 words?+
4,000 words takes 16 minutes 48 seconds silently at 238 WPM. Reading aloud at 130 WPM takes 30 minutes 46 seconds. This is typical for academic papers, comprehensive guides, and long-form sales pages.
How long does it take to read 5,000 words?+
5,000 words takes 21 minutes exactly silently at 238 WPM. Reading aloud takes 38 minutes 28 seconds at 130 WPM. This is the typical length of a detailed white paper, comprehensive tutorial, or short eBook chapter.
