About WordsToTime

WordsToTime is a free tool for anyone who needs to know how long it will take to deliver, read, or present a written document. Built to solve a simple problem: writers, speakers, educators, and creators constantly need to know "how long will this take?"

Data Sources

  • 130 WPM average speaking rate — American Speech-Language-Hearing Association (ASHA)
  • 238 WPM average silent reading rate — ASHA
  • 183 WPM average read-aloud rate — ASHA
  • 163 WPM TED Talk average — TED Talk guidelines
  • Debate timing standards — National Speech & Debate Association (NSDA)

Free Forever

WordsToTime is completely free — no signup, no hidden fees, no premium tier. Supported by Google AdSense advertising. We do not collect personal data or require registration.
