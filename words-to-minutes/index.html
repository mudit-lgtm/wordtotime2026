Frequently Asked Questions

How many minutes is 1,000 words?+
At an average speaking pace of 130 WPM, 1,000 words takes 7 minutes 41 seconds. At slow pace (110 WPM): 9 minutes 5 seconds. At fast pace (150 WPM): 6 minutes 40 seconds. Silent reading (238 WPM): 4 minutes 12 seconds. See our 1,000 words to minutes page for full details.
How long does it take to say 500 words?+
500 words takes 3 minutes 51 seconds at average speaking pace of 130 WPM. At slow pace (110 WPM): 4 minutes 33 seconds. At fast pace (150 WPM): 3 minutes 20 seconds. Visit our 500 words to minutes page for a detailed guide.
How many words is a 5-minute speech?+
A 5-minute speech requires approximately 650 words at an average speaking rate of 130 WPM. At slow pace (110 WPM): ~550 words. At fast pace (150 WPM): ~750 words. See our 5-minute speech word count guide.
How do I convert words to minutes?+
Use the formula: Time (seconds) = Word Count ÷ WPM × 60. Example: 1,000 words ÷ 130 WPM × 60 = 461 seconds = 7 minutes 41 seconds. Or simply use the free calculator above and get results instantly.
How many minutes is 1,500 words?+
1,500 words takes 11 minutes 32 seconds at average speaking pace of 130 WPM. At 110 WPM (slow): 13 minutes 38 seconds. At 150 WPM (fast): 10 minutes exactly. This is a common length for blog posts and short speeches.
How many minutes is 2,000 words?+
2,000 words takes 15 minutes 23 seconds at average speaking pace of 130 WPM. At 110 WPM: 18 minutes 11 seconds. Silent reading (238 WPM): 8 minutes 24 seconds. This is typical for a medium-length presentation.
How many minutes is 600 words?+
600 words takes 4 minutes 37 seconds at average speaking pace of 130 WPM. At 110 WPM: 5 minutes 27 seconds. At 150 WPM: 4 minutes exactly. See our 600 words to minutes page.
What is the average speaking rate in words per minute?+
The average speaking rate is 130 WPM according to the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association (ASHA). Formal speeches: 120–140 WPM. TED Talks: ~163 WPM. Slow/deliberate: 100–120 WPM. Conversational: 150–170 WPM. Silent reading: ~238 WPM.
How many minutes is 400 words?+
400 words takes 3 minutes 5 seconds at 130 WPM. At slow pace (110 WPM): 3 minutes 38 seconds. At fast pace (150 WPM): 2 minutes 40 seconds. See our 400 words to minutes page.
How many minutes is 300 words?+
300 words takes 2 minutes 18 seconds at 130 WPM. At slow pace (110 WPM): 2 minutes 44 seconds. At fast pace (150 WPM): 2 minutes exactly. Visit our 300 words to minutes page for details.
How many words in a 10-minute speech?+
A 10-minute speech requires approximately 1,300 words at 130 WPM. At slow pace (110 WPM): ~1,100 words. At fast pace (150 WPM): ~1,500 words. See the full 10-minute speech word count guide.
Is this calculator accurate for presentations?+
Yes — for scripted presentations, the calculator is highly accurate when you use the right WPM. For formal presentations, use 120–130 WPM. Always add 10–15% buffer for pauses, questions, and nerves. Use our live practice mode to test your actual delivery speed.
