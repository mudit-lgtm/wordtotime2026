Frequently Asked Questions

At an average speaking pace of 130 WPM, 1,000 words takes 7 minutes 41 seconds. At 110 WPM (slow): 9m 5s. At 150 WPM (fast): 6m 40s.

500 words takes 3 minutes 51 seconds at 130 WPM. At 110 WPM: 4m 33s. At 150 WPM: 3m 20s. See our 500 words to minutes page.

1,500 words takes 11 minutes 32 seconds at 130 WPM. At 110 WPM: 13m 38s. At 150 WPM: 10m 0s.

2,000 words takes 15 minutes 23 seconds at 130 WPM. Silent reading takes 8m 24s at 238 WPM.

The formula is: Time (seconds) = Word Count ÷ WPM × 60. For 1,000 words at 130 WPM: 1,000 ÷ 130 × 60 = 461 seconds = 7m 41s.

For formal speeches, aim for 120–140 WPM. TED Talks average 163 WPM. Conversational pace is 150–170 WPM. Slow, deliberate speeches use 100–120 WPM.
