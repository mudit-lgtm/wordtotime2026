📚 Writing Your 4-Minute Speech: Complete Guide

How many words is a 4-minute speech?

A 4-minute speech is 520 words at the standard 130 WPM rate recommended by the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association (ASHA). This is the most widely cited benchmark for public speaking, presentations, and prepared speeches. The practical range for a 4-minute speech is 440–650 words, depending on your natural speaking speed. Use the public speaking calculator to find your exact word target at any WPM.

Structure of a 4-minute speech

At 520 words, a 4-minute speech has limited space. Use this structure for maximum impact:

  • Opening hook (30 sec / ~65 words) — one compelling story, statistic or question
  • Main point 1 (60 sec / ~130 words) — your strongest argument with one example
  • Main point 2 (60 sec / ~130 words) — supporting evidence or contrasting perspective
  • Main point 3 (45 sec / ~97 words) — actionable takeaway or emotional appeal
  • Closing call-to-action (45 sec / ~98 words) — memorable final statement + call to act

This is exactly how Toastmasters International structures their Ice Breaker speeches, which run 4–6 minutes.

Practice tip: Record yourself and compare against the 4-minute timer above. Most speakers discover they speak 10–20% faster when nervous — so if you clock 3:30 in practice, you will likely hit 4:00 during delivery. Aim for 3:45–3:55 in rehearsal.

How many words is a 4-5 minute speech?

A 4–5 minute speech requires 520–650 words at 130 WPM. At conversational 150 WPM: 600–750 words. For a comfortable mid-point, aim for 560–600 words — this puts you safely within the 4–5 minute window at any speaking rate between 120 and 160 WPM. See our 5-minute speech guide for the upper end of this range.

How many words is a 3-4 minute speech?

A 3–4 minute speech requires 390–520 words at 130 WPM. At conversational 150 WPM: 450–600 words. For a safe 3–4 minute range, write 450–480 words — this gives you flexibility to slow down on key points without running over 4 minutes. See the 3-minute speech guide for the lower end.

Toastmasters note: Toastmasters Ice Breaker speeches are 4–6 minutes (520–780 words at 130 WPM). The green light turns on at 4 minutes, yellow at 5 minutes, red at 6 minutes. Aim for 560–620 words to peak in the green-to-yellow zone.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many words is a 4 minute speech?+
A 4-minute speech is 520 words at the standard public speaking rate of 130 WPM (ASHA). At conversational 150 WPM, a 4-minute speech is 600 words. At TED Talk pace (163 WPM), it is 652 words. At a slow, deliberate pace (110 WPM), a 4-minute speech is 440 words. The practical range for most speakers is 480–600 words. Use the public speaking calculator for your specific speaking rate.
How long is a 4 minute speech?+
A 4-minute speech is exactly 240 seconds. At 130 WPM (ASHA standard), this equals 520 words. The felt length depends heavily on pacing — a slow, deliberate speaker delivering 440 words fills the same 4 minutes as a fast speaker delivering 720 words. Use the 4-minute countdown timer above to practice staying within your slot. Add 10–15% buffer time for natural pauses and emphasis.
How many pages is a 4 minute speech?+
A 4-minute speech at 520 words (130 WPM) is approximately 1.9 pages double-spaced in standard academic format (12pt Times New Roman, 1-inch margins). Single-spaced, it is roughly 1 full page. At 600 words (150 WPM), a 4-minute speech is 2.2 pages double-spaced. Most speakers round up to 2 pages for easy reading from a printed script.
How many words is a 4–5 minute speech?+
A 4–5 minute speech is 520–650 words at 130 WPM (ASHA standard). At 150 WPM (conversational): 600–750 words. For a comfortable mid-range delivery, aim for 560–600 words — this puts you within a 4–5 minute window at any speaking rate between 120 and 160 WPM. See the 5-minute speech guide for the upper end.
How many words is a 3–4 minute speech?+
A 3–4 minute speech is 390–520 words at 130 WPM. At 150 WPM: 450–600 words. For a safe 3–4 minute range, write 450–480 words — this gives you flexibility to slow down on key points without running over. See the 3-minute speech guide for a full breakdown.
How many words is a 4 minute presentation?+
A 4-minute presentation is 520 words at 130 WPM (ASHA). Presentations typically run slightly slower than pure speeches due to pauses for slides — aim for 480–500 words for a 4-minute presentation to allow natural transition pauses. Use the presentation time calculator for slide-by-slide timing.
