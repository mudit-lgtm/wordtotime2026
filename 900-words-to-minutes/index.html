📚 900 Words — Full Context Guide

How long is 900 words as a speech?

A 900-word speech lasts 6 minutes 55 seconds at the standard formal speaking rate of 130 WPM, as defined by the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association (ASHA). This makes a 900-word script ideal for a 7-minute presentation slot — giving you a small buffer for pauses, transitions, and emphasis. If your slot is exactly 7 minutes, 900 words at 130 WPM fits perfectly. At a conversational pace of 150 WPM, the same 900 words takes exactly 6 minutes flat.

Practical Rule: 900 words ÷ 130 WPM = 6.92 minutes = 6 minutes 55 seconds. To target a specific time slot, multiply your target minutes × 130 to get word count. A 7-minute speech = 910 words at 130 WPM.

Is 900 words a 7-minute speech?

Yes — at 130 WPM (formal pace), 900 words = 6 min 55 sec, which rounds to a 7-minute speech. At Toastmasters' recommended range (120–160 WPM), a 900-word script runs between 5m 37s and 7m 30s. If you tend to speak quickly (150–163 WPM), your 900-word speech will clock in at 5m 31s–6m 00s — closer to a 6-minute speech. Rehearse with our Practice Mode timer to find your actual pace.

For Toastmasters Speeches: Standard Toastmasters speeches require 5–7 minutes. At 130 WPM, a 5-minute speech = 650 words, a 7-minute speech = 910 words. A 900-word script is right at the 7-minute upper limit — perfect for Ice Breaker or Project speeches.

How long does it take to read 900 words?

Reading 900 words silently takes 3 minutes 47 seconds at the average adult reading speed of 238 WPM. Reading 900 words aloud takes 4 minutes 30 seconds at 200 WPM. Slow readers (150 WPM) take 6 minutes. Advanced readers (300 WPM) finish in just 3 minutes. For blog posts and articles, 900 words is typically shown as a "4-minute read" on platforms like Medium. Use our reading time calculator for precise estimates.

How long does it take to type 900 words?

Typing 900 words takes 16 minutes 22 seconds for an average typist (55 WPM). Slow typists at 40 WPM need 22 minutes 30 seconds. Fast typists at 80 WPM complete it in 11 minutes 15 seconds. Professionals at 100 WPM type 900 words in exactly 9 minutes. These estimates are for continuous typing — real-world writing includes thinking time, which typically doubles or triples the total time. For pure typing speed estimates, use the typing time calculator.

900 Words as Content: 900 words is a medium blog post (3–4 min read), a 7-minute speech, a 3-page double-spaced essay (250 words/page), or about 1.8 pages single-spaced. It takes the average person about 45–60 minutes to write 900 original words.
