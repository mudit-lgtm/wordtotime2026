📚 1200 Words — Complete Guide

How long does it take to say 1200 words?

1200 words takes 9 minutes 14 seconds to say at 130 WPM (ASHA formal speech standard). The most useful benchmark: at exactly 120 WPM, 1200 words = precisely 10 minutes — making it the ideal word count for a 10-minute presentation or class assignment. At conversational 150 WPM, 1200 words takes exactly 8 minutes. The multiple clean-number calculations (6, 8, 10, 12 minutes) make 1200 words one of the most versatile word counts for timed speeches. Don't know your WPM? Use the WPM self-test to measure your natural speaking speed and return to this calculator.

How long does it take to read 1200 words?

1200 words takes 5 minutes 3 seconds to read silently at the average adult reading speed of 238 WPM (Brysbaert et al., 2019). At 300 WPM (above-average reader) it takes exactly 4 minutes. At a slow 200 WPM it takes exactly 6 minutes. Reading time estimates on blogs and news sites typically use 200–250 WPM, so a "6-minute read" or "5-minute read" tag on a 1200-word article is accurate depending on the platform's assumed speed. For academic text (denser, more complex vocabulary), use 150–200 WPM as the reading speed estimate. Check any article's reading time with the reading time calculator.

10-minute presentation at 120 WPM: If you have a 10-minute presentation slot, write exactly 1200 words and practise speaking at 120 WPM — a deliberate, clear pace that audiences find easy to follow. This is slightly slower than the ASHA standard (130 WPM) but creates a calmer, more authoritative delivery. Leave the final 30–60 seconds for Q&A setup rather than rushing to fill the full 10 minutes with content.

How long does it take to read 1200 words out loud?

Reading 1200 words out loud takes 9 minutes 14 seconds at 130 WPM — the same as speaking pace, because oral reading speed equals speaking speed. Out-loud reading is 60–70% slower than silent reading. Most people read aloud at 120–140 WPM, giving a range of 8m 34s to 10m 00s for 1200 words out loud. Teachers often ask students to "read your essay aloud" as a timing check — at 120–130 WPM, a 1200-word essay fills a 9–10 minute oral assignment slot.

How many pages is 1200 words?

1200 words is 4.8 pages double-spaced (250 words per page, standard academic formatting: 12pt Times New Roman, 1-inch margins). Single-spaced, it is 2.4 pages. As a blog post, 1200 words typically fills 4–5 scrolled screens on a desktop monitor. 1200 words is the sweet spot for long-form blog content — substantial enough to cover a topic in depth, short enough that most readers will scroll to the end. For context: this word count is typical for short undergraduate essays, detailed email newsletters, op-eds, and introductory how-to articles. See the full reading time calculator for any word count.
